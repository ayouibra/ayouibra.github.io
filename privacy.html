Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Parlane Sole Prop (dba Parlane) collects, uses, and protects information when you call a restaurant that uses Parlane’s automated phone-ordering assistant.

What we collect

  • Phone number of the person calling, from caller ID or as spoken on the call.
  • Order details you give on the call: items, notes, and the first name for the order.
  • Call recordings and transcripts, used to process the order and as a record of your consent to receive texts.
  • Text message replies you send to us, such as YES, STOP, or HELP.

How we use it

  • To place your order with the restaurant you called.
  • If you agree on the call, to text you a summary of your order to confirm (and an updated summary if you change it) and a confirmation with your order number. Up to 4 messages per order.
  • To respond to HELP requests and honor STOP requests.
  • To fix problems and improve order accuracy.

We do not use your phone number for marketing.

Who we share it with

We share order details with the restaurant you called so it can prepare your order. We use service providers, such as our telephony and SMS provider, only to deliver the service.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Text-messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates.

Retention

We keep order records and call recordings only as long as needed to operate the service and resolve disputes, then delete them.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. Reply START to resubscribe.
  • Reply HELP for help.
  • To ask about or delete your data, email ibrahim.am1998@gmail.com.
