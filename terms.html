SMS Terms

Program: Parlane Order Texts, operated by Parlane Sole Prop.

What you’ll receive: When you call a restaurant that uses Parlane’s phone-ordering assistant and agree on the call to receive texts, we send transactional messages about that order only: a summary of your order that you can confirm by replying YES (updated if you change the order), a confirmation with your order number once the order is placed, or a notice if the call ended before the order was placed.
